## 🔴 Mutual liability cap with standard carve-outs: **RED** (missing)

**Why:** The agreement contains no mutual cap on aggregate liability tied to fees paid or payable. The closest provisions are a consequential and liquidated damages waiver limited to late delivery (c23) and a Company-only exclusion for defective Products (c40). Because no cap clause exists where one is expected, this is red for missing.

## 🔴 Client owns deliverables and work product: **RED** (missing)

**Why:** This is a distributor agreement, not a development or services agreement, but the golden rule still applies: Client should own what it creates. Section 3.2 only requires Distributor to disclose proposed product improvements and bars it from making modifications without consent. Section 5.5 disclaims any implied licence, but only for Company's IP. Because no clause addresses ownership of Distributor-created deliverables or work product, the rule is missing.
